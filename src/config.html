Webhook

Send notes from your watch to any URL.

Destinations

Where messages can go. Add one for each service. Each new host asks for your permission the first time it's used.

Voice notes

On the watchapp, press Select and speak.

Button commands

Send a fixed command with Up or Down on the watchapp. Leave a command empty to turn that button off.

The reminder is shown on the watch with an arrow pointing at the button. Write whatever helps you remember what it does.

Timeline pin

Adds a "Send note" pin to your watch's timeline. Open it, say your note, and it goes to your voice-note destination.
